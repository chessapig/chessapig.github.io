  - image: pseudosphere
    title:  pseudopseudosphere
    caption:  To quickly make a pseudosphere, build many cones of the same radius but different cone angles. Then, stack them in order of angle. In the limit of many cones, the cross section is a Tractrix, so the result is the surface of revolution of a tractrix. It just so happens that this has constant negative curvature! I learned about this from Fran Herr, who says it goes back to Thurston (like all good things). The innovation here is to not tape the cones together. Instead, snip off their tops, and stack them on a little rod. Then, we can bend the rod this way and that, and have so much fun.

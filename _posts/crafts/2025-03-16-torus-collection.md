  - image: christmas
    title: "Spiral papercut torus"
    caption: |
      I made this torus with cardstock and a lot of patience. Its made entirely out of many copies of [this template](/assets/crafts/tori/cutout.pdf), cut out of cardstock. I used a papercutter machine to cut the paper, but scissors should work fine. 
      
      The torus is constructed out of two spirals, one green and one red, one rotating clockwise and the other counterclockwise. These weave between one another, interfacing using the provided notches. 
      <div class="text-center"> <img src="/assets/crafts/tori/expanded.jpeg" alt="slinky-like stack of spirals of paper. Soon to become a torus. " width="400" > </div>
      
      I kept adding layers until I could comfertably wrap the stack around into a circle. It took about 50 layers of each color. Here's what the accordian looks like all compressed.
      <div class="text-center"> <img src="/assets/crafts/tori/compressed.jpeg" alt="thick stack of circular paper, about 4 inches thick " width="400" > </div>
      
      The final torus is fun to hold because it rotates around its axis very well. If you just grab the outside, it can wriggle from your grasp like one of those water tube toys.

    title: "Clay torus"
    caption: |
      I spun this one on a potters wheel (a tool designed specifically for surfaces of revolution). I still haven't fired it, fingers crossed that it will survive the endevor.
  - image: heinz
    title: "Hopf fibration"
    caption: |
      A bracelet I found in the thrift store. It's made of 100 metal rings, each interlocking with every other ring. Topologically, the rings are all fibers of the Hopf fibration. If you hold all the rings together, they form a filled torus.
  - image: woven
    title: "Paper woven torus"
    caption: |
      Successful paper torus, woven out of many circles. This weaving pattern is not topopolgically regular. The blank spaces on the inside and outside of the wheel are 4-sided, while on the front and back they are only 2 sided. Also, the paper doesn't conform to the surface of the torus like one might want. Still, its nice and quite studry. This is the prototype for the PVC construction of the skeleton of my Halloween costume, see next picture.

    title: "Horuse skeleton"

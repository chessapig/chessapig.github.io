summary: I describe how to quantize Chern-Simons theory for an abelian gauge group. In particular, I describe the Hilbert space which Chern-Simons assigns to a surface. I follow the geometric quantization procedure, where the Hilbert space is the space of holomorphic sections over the moduli space of line bundles. I describe this space explicitly using theta functions. Next, I describe how the quantization depends on the choice of complex structure on our surface. I describe the projective connection on the bundle of Hilbert spaces, and its relationship with the heat equation.
